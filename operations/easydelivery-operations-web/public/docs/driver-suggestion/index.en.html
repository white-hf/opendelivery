Driver Suggestions

1. Map Positioning & Driving Navigation

During delivery runs, we need the map to dynamically adjust its camera angle based on our driving state and real-time location:

1.1 Map-Centric Workspace & Route Overview

In our daily workflow, except for the few tens of minutes spent scanning packages at the warehouse in the morning, we spend the vast majority of our time on the road driving and delivering. Therefore, the app should be dominated by a map interface, providing a visual experience similar to Google Navigation, that visually plots all packages as markers rather than listing them on text-only pages.

【Driver Benefit】: Since most of our time post-loading is spent on the road, a map-centric design makes it significantly easier to operate and check package locations while driving, eliminating the constant need to switch back and forth between a list and the map.

1.2 Navigation-Level Auto-Zoom

When the map is in follow mode, the camera zoom scale should dynamically adapt to the vehicle's speed. As speed increases, the camera zooms out to display a broader traffic view; as we slow down, it zooms in to reveal local details.

【Driver Benefit】: For drivers who deliver in residential neighborhoods and are already familiar with the area, the map camera automatically zooming in to show street layouts allows us to navigate directly without having to launch external third-party navigation apps, reducing app-switching overhead.

1.3 Driver Location Centered

When routing or crawling at low speed to locate a house number, our blue location cursor must stay locked at the exact center of the map, keeping the viewport aligned with our movement.

【Driver Benefit】: Ensures our current location is always the primary focus, preventing the need to manually swipe or drag the screen to find ourselves while driving, allowing us to keep both hands on the wheel.

1.4 Doorstep Auto-Zoom & Image Stability

As we approach and pull up to the customer's house, the map should automatically zoom in and flatten its angle to present a clear door-side close-up. During this transition, the map viewport must remain completely stable, avoiding any lens jitter or rapid zooming as we decelerate or stop.

【Driver Benefit】: The moment the vehicle stops, we immediately get a stable, clear indicator of the target door. We can verify the building and dismount instantly without waiting for the camera to stabilize or having to manually adjust the scale.

2. Proof of Delivery & Nearby Quick-Switch

When walking up to drop off a parcel, we need to minimize the number of clicks required on the screen:

2.1 One-Step Scan and Photo Proof (POD)

When taking a photo at the customer's door, the moment the driver points the phone at the parcel's barcode, the system automatically detects and compares the barcode. If it matches, the photo capture of the shipping label is automatically completed; if it doesn't match, the driver is prompted that they have grabbed the wrong package, eliminating the need to manually tap to take the photo.

【Driver Benefit】: Saves 2 button presses per package. Over a 150-package day, this cuts down 300 screen taps, minimizing time spent idling at the doorstep.

2.2 Quick-Switch for Nearby Parcels

Within the photo screen, if the app detects other packages destined for the same building or nearby addresses, the interface should directly pop up a list of nearby packages (via a Fragment) and support switching the camera interface directly to the next target with one tap, instead of exiting to the home map after every delivery.

【Driver Benefit】: When multiple packages are at the same location or nearby, we can switch targets directly on the camera screen, greatly reducing the need to exit, re-select, and enter pages repeatedly in the App.

2.3 Delivery Process & Photo Interface Simplification

Actual Scenario & Pain Point: Currently, going from clicking a package list item or map marker to completing a delivery requires 6-7 clicks. For example, clicking a marker opens a details screen (which redundantly includes a map view), then clicking "Delivered" opens yet another details page, and only after clicking the camera button do we finally reach the photo screen. These two intermediate pages display excessive, repeating information and create unnecessary steps.

Driver Request: We want to consolidate or bypass these redundant screens. Clicking a package marker or list item should take us directly to a simplified photo taking interface containing only the most critical package details (such as suite/tracking number) and action buttons, letting us complete the entire delivery in just 2-3 clicks (or within 3 clicks for apartments that require multiple photos).

【Driver Benefit】: Drastically reduces repetitive screen navigation and taps at the doorstep, preventing us from getting bogged down in complex menus and speeding up individual deliveries.

3. Offline Delivery Experience

When driving through remote no-signal areas or during temporary server outages, the application's core functionality must not be interrupted. Parcel scanning, status updates, and photo capture must function normally and respond instantly, ensuring our delivery workflow remains completely unaffected.

4. Driver Utility Tools

To help with daily sorting, performance review, and apartment deliveries, we need a few practical utility tools:

4.1 Large Parcel Identification

Actual Scenario & Pain Point: After sorting goods at the warehouse, we organize packages in sequence of delivery route numbers into individual bags or bins of 10 to 20 items, placing these containers in designated spots in the van. However, large or heavy boxes cannot fit in these bags and are placed separately in the trunk or open spaces. When delivering sequentially, if we search for a package in the current route bag and cannot find it, we get confused and waste time digging through the container, only to later realize it is actually a large item stored in the trunk.

Functional Requirement: We should be able to tag parcels as "Large" during loading. Packages marked as large must display a clear "Large Parcel" badge on the map marker or package details page.

【Driver Benefit】: When delivering sequentially, as soon as we see the Large badge on the map marker or details page, we immediately know not to waste time searching the route bag. Instead, we can go straight to the trunk, saving futile search time.

4.2 Delivery Count & Daily Mileage Tracker

Functional Requirement: The system should provide a calendar summary showing our daily delivered package count and GPS-calculated mileage, supporting down-drill tracking to review delivery waybills for any selected day.

【Driver Benefit】: Allows us to quickly verify our daily delivery count at the end of a shift. Since our piecemeal earnings are tied directly to package counts, and vehicle mileage is required for claiming business vehicle tax deductions on our personal tax returns, this provides an accurate, leak-proof local log for payroll verification and tax filings.

4.3 Historical Delivery Photos

Actual Scenario & Functional Requirement: When new drivers visit unfamiliar addresses, they often struggle to locate the correct spot because the layout is complex or the building lacks clear physical door numbers. The app should store and display photos taken during past successful deliveries at that address, helping drivers verify the correct spot by comparing the building's physical appearance.

【Driver Benefit】: Helps new drivers quickly locate target doors in complex environments or unnumbered houses, preventing misdeliveries and saving us from having to call customers for directions.

4.4 Apartment Unit Number Auto-completion & Photo Proof

Actual Scenario & Functional Requirement: During apartment building deliveries, customers often omit their specific unit or suite numbers. We want the system to automatically determine and fill in the missing apartment number based on successful historical delivery records for that address/recipient name, while displaying past delivery photos on the screen to verify the target mailbox or door.

【Driver Benefit】: Target units can be verified quickly without having to wait in building lobbies or call customers. Backed by automatic completion and historical photos, we can carry the package straight upstairs, reducing return rates and boosting apartment delivery speed.

4.5 Smart Battery Saving & Location Control

Actual Scenario & Functional Requirement: We run the app for long shifts, and GPS tracking drains the phone battery quickly. We need the system to be smart: when location tracking is not actively required (e.g., when parked or scanning packages at a doorstep), it should automatically pause GPS updates to reduce background power draw and device heat.

【Driver Benefit】: Reduces phone heating during high-intensity scanning/photo tasks and extends battery life, ensuring our phone stays powered throughout the entire shift without relying on power banks.
